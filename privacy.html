Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Ovora is a fertility-awareness app built around the Billings Ovulation Method. It records intimate health information, so this page says plainly what is kept, where it goes, and how to destroy it. Everything below describes what the app actually does.

The short version

  • Your chart lives on your phone. There is no Ovora account, no email, no password.
  • If you connect a partner, a copy is mirrored through Google Firestore so their phone can display it. It is encrypted on your device before it is sent. We hold ciphertext; we cannot read it, and neither can Google.
  • We do not sell your data, share it with data brokers, or use it for advertising. There is no tracking or advertising SDK in the app.
  • Delete everything in Settings removes the copy on your phone, the encrypted copy on the server, and the anonymous identifier it was filed under.

What Ovora stores

On your device. Your daily observations (sensation, appearance, bleeding, intercourse, temperature, LH test results, notes), the symptoms you log, your cycle history, and your settings — including your goal, charting method, reminder time and language.

On our server (Google Firestore), only if you connect a partner. The same chart and profile, encrypted end-to-end on your device before it is sent. The server stores an unreadable blob. The decryption key is held on your phone and shared only with the partner device you deliberately paired with, using a code you chose to give them.

An anonymous identifier. Ovora signs your install in to Firebase anonymously. This produces a random identifier that names your documents. It is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, or any account you hold anywhere else.

Nothing else. Ovora does not collect your contacts, your location, your photos, your device's advertising identifier, or your browsing activity.

Nothing leaves your device for any purpose other than making a feature work. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no profiling.

Connecting a partner

Pairing is entirely your choice and is started by you sharing a code. While connected, the partner device can read your chart. You can disconnect at any time from the connection screen; the link is removed on both devices and the partner's mirrored copy stops updating.

Your partner never sees your notes to yourself, and cannot log or change anything on your chart.

Backups

Ovora can write a backup file, either when you ask or automatically to a folder you choose. That file is yours: it is written to storage you selected, and Ovora never uploads it anywhere.

Manual backups can be password-protected. Automatic backups are not encrypted — a background task cannot ask you for a password — so choose a folder only you can reach. The app says this where you pick the folder.

Deleting your data

Settings → About & data → Delete everything. You type DELETE to confirm. This removes:

  • everything stored on your phone,
  • the encrypted copy on the server, including every logged day and your profile,
  • any pairing code you created, and
  • the anonymous identifier itself.

It cannot be undone and we cannot recover it. Backup files you have already saved are yours and are not touched — delete those yourself if you want them gone.

Deleting the app without using Delete everything first removes the copy on your phone. If you would like the server copy destroyed as well, use Delete everything before uninstalling, or contact us at the address below.

Who else is involved

Ovora uses three third parties, each only for the function named:

  • Google Firebase / Firestore — stores the encrypted mirror and issues the anonymous identifier. Google's privacy policy
  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions. RevenueCat's privacy policy
  • Google Gemini API — answers Coach questions. Google's privacy policy

Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google. Ovora never sees your card details.

How long anything is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or remove the app. The encrypted server mirror stays until you delete everything, or until you disconnect and delete your data. We do not keep backups of your chart on our side.

Children

Ovora is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live).

Your rights

Because your chart is on your device and the server copy is unreadable to us, the most complete and immediate way to access, export or erase your data is inside the app: use Download backup to export, and Delete everything to erase. If you are in a region with statutory data rights and want to exercise them through us instead, write to the address below.

Medical disclaimer

Ovora supports charting. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose, and does not replace a doctor or a qualified Billings Ovulation Method tutor. No app can guarantee the prevention or achievement of pregnancy.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and note the change in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy, about the data we hold, or a request to have the server copy destroyed — write to hi@ovora.app and we will answer.
