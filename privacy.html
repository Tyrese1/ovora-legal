Ovora

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Ovora is a fertility-awareness app built around the Billings Ovulation Method. It records intimate health information, so this page says plainly what is kept, where it goes, and how to destroy it. Everything below describes what the app actually does.

The short version

  • Your chart lives on your phone. There is no Ovora account, no email, no password.
  • A copy of your chart is also kept on our server (Google Firestore), under a random, anonymous ID. This happens for everyone, whether or not you connect a partner. It is encrypted on your phone before it is sent, with a key that stays on your phone.
  • If you never pair with a partner, that key never leaves your phone, and nobody (us included) can read the copy on our server.
  • If you pair with a partner, your key is shared through our server, locked with your pairing code, so that their phone can read your chart. Read Pairing below for what that means.
  • We do not sell your data, share it with data brokers, or use it for advertising. There is no advertising or cross-app tracking in the app. Optional diagnostics are described below.
  • Delete everything in Settings removes the copy on your phone, the encrypted copy on the server, and the anonymous identifier it was filed under.

What Ovora stores

On your device. Your daily observations (sensation, appearance, bleeding, intercourse, temperature, LH test results, notes), the symptoms you log, your cycle history, and your settings — including your goal, charting method, reminder time and language.

On our server (Google Firestore), for every install. A copy of your daily observations and of your profile: your name, settings, goal, cycle details, and your symptom and mood diary. It is encrypted on your phone before it is sent (AES-256), so what reaches the server is unreadable without your key. Each day is stored as its own entry named by its date. That means the server can see which days you logged, and when entries were last updated, but not what you logged. The copy is kept so that a partner can be connected at any time and see your whole chart straight away, and so that Delete everything can remove it.

This copy is not a backup. The key that reads it stays on your phone, so if you lose your phone or delete the app, the copy can't be used to restore your chart. Keep a backup file for that (see Backups).

Your encryption key. It is created on your phone and kept there, protected by the phone's secure storage (the Android Keystore or the iOS Keychain). If you never create a pairing code, it never leaves your phone.

Pairing. When you create a pairing code, a copy of your key, locked with that code, is stored on our server in a pairing record. The record also holds the code itself, your anonymous ID, your partner's anonymous ID once they connect, and when each phone was last active. Our security rules let only the two paired phones read it, so other Ovora users can't get your key. However, because the code is kept alongside the locked key, the pairing record does not stop someone with administrative access to our database (us, or our hosting provider) from unlocking your chart. We do not do this.

An anonymous identifier. Ovora signs your install in to Firebase anonymously. This produces a random identifier that names your documents. It is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, or any account you hold anywhere else.

Nothing else. Ovora does not collect your contacts, your location, your photos, your device's advertising identifier, or your browsing activity.

Optional diagnostics and analytics

New versions of Ovora ask whether you want to share diagnostics. Collection is off until you allow it. You can change your choice in Settings → Privacy & data → Help improve Ovora. This choice does not affect your chart or subscription.

If allowed, Firebase receives technical crash reports (including stack traces, app version, device and operating-system information), installation identifiers, and basic interaction events: onboarding, first saved log, paywall viewing, and an active trial or paid plan observed by the app. These events do not include cycle days, symptoms, observations, your goal, names, notes, or chat messages. Ovora does not attach its Firebase Authentication identifier to analytics or crash reports. Analytics is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Turning diagnostics off stops new collection, resets the local analytics identifier and deletes unsent crash reports. It does not erase reports already received by Firebase; these remain subject to the provider's retention policies. Likewise, Delete everything removes your chart/account data, not previously submitted aggregate analytics.

Remote Config retrieves public app settings, such as supported builds and feature availability. It does not change your paid entitlement or the price charged by Apple or Google. App Check uses platform attestation to verify that requests come from a registered app; it is separate from optional analytics and remains active to protect the service.

Optional Apple Health and Health Connect access

In versions with Settings → Data sources, you may choose to view steps, sleep, weight and recorded period dates from Apple Health on iOS or Health Connect on Android. Access is off until you choose categories and complete the phone's permission screen. You may decline any category and continue using Ovora.

This connection is read-only. Readings are requested while you use the Data sources screen, held temporarily in memory, and displayed on your device. They are not written into your Ovora chart or used to change fertility predictions. They are not sent to your partner, Coach, translation services, diagnostics, analytics, advertising, or our servers; they are not included in Ovora backups. We save only your selected categories locally, not the readings. Ovora does not write or delete records in Apple Health or Health Connect.

Disconnect stops Ovora reading these sources and clears its local choices. On Android it also asks Health Connect to revoke Ovora's permissions. On iOS, revoke system permissions in Apple Health → your profile → Apps → Ovora. You can manage either platform's permissions directly in its health settings at any time. Delete everything also disables Ovora's local connection. Disconnecting or resetting Ovora does not remove the original records stored by Apple Health, Health Connect or the apps that created them.

Health records are never used for advertising, marketing, data brokerage or unrelated profiling. The health platform and source apps retain their own data under their respective policies.

Connecting a partner

Pairing is entirely your choice and is started by you sharing a code. While connected, the partner device can read your chart. You can disconnect at any time from the connection screen; the link is removed on both devices and the partner's mirrored copy stops updating.

Your partner never sees your notes to yourself, and cannot log or change anything on your chart.

Backups

Ovora can write a backup file, either when you ask or automatically to a folder you choose. That file is yours: it is written to storage you selected, and Ovora never uploads it anywhere.

Manual backups can be password-protected. Automatic backups are not encrypted — a background task cannot ask you for a password — so choose a folder only you can reach. The app says this where you pick the folder.

Deleting your data

Settings → About & data → Delete everything. You type DELETE to confirm. This removes:

  • everything stored on your phone,
  • the encrypted copy on the server, including every logged day and your profile,
  • any pairing code you created, and
  • the anonymous identifier itself.

It cannot be undone and we cannot recover it. Backup files you have already saved are yours and are not touched — delete those yourself if you want them gone.

Deleting the app without using Delete everything first removes the copy on your phone. If you would like the server copy destroyed as well, use Delete everything before uninstalling, or contact us at the address below.

Apple devices (iPhone and iPad)

On iOS, Ovora works with these Apple features. None of them sends your chart to Apple.

  • Purchases. Ovora Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple takes the payment and handles your Apple Account and card details under Apple's privacy policy; Ovora never sees them. To confirm that Ovora Plus is active, RevenueCat receives the App Store purchase information for the transaction, along with an anonymous identifier that RevenueCat generates. It is not your name, email or Apple Account.
  • App lock. If you turn on the app lock, Ovora asks iOS to check your Face ID, Touch ID or device passcode. iOS does the matching itself. Ovora is told only whether it passed, and never receives, stores or sends your face, fingerprint or passcode.
  • Keychain. Your encryption key is held in the iOS Keychain, set to stay on this device only. It is not synced to other devices and is not included in device backups.
  • Reminders. Reminder notifications are scheduled on your phone by iOS. Ovora does not register with the Apple Push Notification service and no reminder content passes through our server.
  • Device backups. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or to a computer, Apple's backup may include Ovora's data on your phone, such as your chart. That backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us. Because the encryption key is not part of it, the encrypted copy on our server still cannot be read from it.
  • Apple Health. Optional read-only access is described in Health connections above. Ovora does not write your chart into Apple Health.
  • What Ovora does not use. Ovora does not use iCloud or Sign in with Apple, and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it does not show Apple's tracking prompt.

Who else is involved

Ovora uses three third parties, each only for the function named:

  • Google Firebase / Firestore — stores the encrypted mirror and issues the anonymous identifier; App Check verifies app requests, Remote Config provides public settings, and optional Crashlytics/Analytics provide diagnostics. Google's privacy policy
  • RevenueCat — manages subscriptions. RevenueCat's privacy policy
  • Google Gemini API — answers Coach questions. Google's privacy policy

Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google. Ovora never sees your card details.

How long anything is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or remove the app. The encrypted copy on our server stays until you use Delete everything. If you remove the app without doing that, the copy stays on the server until you ask us to delete it. We do not keep backups of your chart on our side.

Children

Ovora is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live).

Your rights

Because your chart is on your device and the server copy is unreadable to us, the most complete and immediate way to access, export or erase your data is inside the app: use Download backup to export, and Delete everything to erase. If you are in a region with statutory data rights and want to exercise them through us instead, write to the address below.

Medical disclaimer

Ovora supports charting. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose, and does not replace a doctor or a qualified Billings Ovulation Method tutor. No app can guarantee the prevention or achievement of pregnancy.
