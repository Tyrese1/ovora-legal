Read your body, not a calendar.

Instead of guessing from dates, Ovora reads your body's own fertility signs. Irregular cycles, breastfeeding and perimenopause don't break it.

Guidance follows your goal: trying to conceive, natural family planning, just tracking your period, or perimenopause.

Learn your patterns.

Cycle lengths, Peak history and mood across the cycle. Period forecasts learn from your last cycles, and alerts count down to what's next.

Forecasts are estimates, not confirmation of ovulation or pregnancy.

Meet Ovo.

A private coach for the questions you'd rather not ask anyone else. Ovo reads an anonymised summary of your chart, never your name, dates or notes.

Responses are educational, not medical advice.

When you're pregnant, Ovora changes with you.

Your week and day, your baby's size from poppy seed to pumpkin, what's developing, and check-in trends, with reads based on NHS guidance.

The estimated due date sits on Today, every week has a short deck of cards, and your cycle history is kept for afterwards.

Connect with care.

Invite your partner with a private code. He sees what matters, in wording written for him, gets his own alerts, and can never edit your chart.

Pairing is optional, and you can disconnect at any time.

Your chart lives on your phone.

Ovora records intimate information, so the rules are simple and written down.

No account.
No email, no password, nothing to sign up for. Your chart is yours from the first day.
Encrypted before it leaves.
A copy is kept on our server so a partner can connect at any time, but it is encrypted on your phone first, with a key that stays there. If you never pair, nobody can read it, us included.
Not for sale.
No advertising, no data brokers, no cross-app tracking.
Delete everything, in one step.
Removes the copy on your phone, the encrypted copy on the server, and the anonymous identifier it was filed under.
